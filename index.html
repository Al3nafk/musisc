    Musisc is a demo made by Alan. It stands on the work of these people and projects.

    Piano sound

    Fluid R3 GM SoundFont by Frank Wen, via midi-js-soundfonts by Benjamin Gleitzman, licensed CC BY 3.0. Trimmed to every third note. The other instrument sounds are made by Musisc in your browser.

    Drawing and timing the music

    abcjs by Paul Rosen and Gregory Dyke (MIT licence).

    Sheet music library

    Mutopia Project (public domain and Creative Commons), OpenScore Lieder (CC0), Wikimedia Commons (free licences shown on each file) and The Session (tunes shared by its community).

    Offline photo and PDF reading

    homr by Christian Liebhardt and contributors (AGPL-3.0, source in library/omr/homr.zip), running on Pyodide (MPL-2.0) and ONNX Runtime Web by Microsoft (MIT). PDFs are opened with PDF.js by Mozilla (Apache-2.0).

    Fonts

    Bricolage Grotesque, Inter and JetBrains Mono, from Google Fonts (SIL Open Font License).
